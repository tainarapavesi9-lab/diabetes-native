What it is

Sugar Reset is a liquid dietary supplement. It is designed to be used alongside a balanced diet, regular activity and the guidance of your doctor. It does not promise miracles, and it is not a replacement for medication or medical care.

Who usually looks at it

Adults over 50 who already watch what they eat, walk most days and work with their doctor, and who want one simple thing to add to the morning routine. It is not a treatment and it does not replace anything your doctor prescribed.

How it is used

According to the label, you shake the bottle well and take 1 to 2 droppers daily, placed in the mouth and swallowed. The company recommends taking it in the morning, preferably before a meal.

Three details people over 50 tend to notice:

  • No pill to swallow. It is a liquid, which matters if you already take several tablets a day.
  • About ten seconds. Shake, one dropper, done, before your coffee.
  • Easy to keep up. One bottle sits on the counter instead of several jars in the cabinet.

What is in the blend, in plain words

  • Chromium, a trace mineral the body uses in small amounts.
  • Gymnema, a leaf used for centuries in Ayurvedic tradition, known there as the "sugar destroyer".
  • Green tea and Panax ginseng, two of the most studied plants in nutrition research.
  • Maca and African mango, traditional plants used for energy and appetite.
  • Amino acids such as L-glutamine, L-arginine and L-carnitine.

The label shows the combined weight of the blend, 200 mg, rather than the amount of each plant.

Where it is made

The label states that the product is "Made in the USA with globally sourced ingredients" and that it "has been manufactured in an FDA registered facility". As with any dietary supplement, these statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration.

What to expect

Supplements are not a quick fix. Companies in this category usually recommend consistent daily use over weeks, alongside a balanced diet and regular activity. Individual results vary, and some people notice nothing at all. That is also why the 60-day guarantee exists.

Before you use it

  • Talk to your doctor first, especially if you have diabetes, high blood pressure or take any medication.
  • The blend contains guarana and green tea, which are sources of caffeine.
  • It contains grapefruit extract, which can interact with some medications, including certain statins and blood pressure drugs.
  • It contains a licorice compound, which at higher amounts can affect blood pressure.
  • Do not use if pregnant or nursing without medical guidance. Keep out of reach of children.
  • Never stop or change your medication on your own.
