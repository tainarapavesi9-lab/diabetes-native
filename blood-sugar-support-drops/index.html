A Liquid Supplement for Daily Blood Sugar Support: How It Works

Many adults over 50 look for simple ways to support healthy habits around blood sugar. Here is a plain look at one liquid supplement, what is on its label and what to consider first.

By the Grayfield Review team · Sponsored · September 15, 2026

What it is

Sugar Reset is a liquid dietary supplement. It is designed to be used alongside a balanced diet, regular activity and the guidance of your doctor. It does not promise miracles, and it is not a replacement for medication or medical care.

How it is used

According to the label, you shake the bottle well and take 1 to 2 droppers daily, placed in the mouth and swallowed. The company recommends taking it in the morning, preferably before a meal.

Before you use it

  • Talk to your doctor first, especially if you have diabetes, high blood pressure or take any medication.
  • The blend contains guarana and green tea, which are sources of caffeine.
  • It contains grapefruit extract, which can interact with some medications, including certain statins and blood pressure drugs.
  • It contains a licorice compound, which at higher amounts can affect blood pressure.
  • Do not use if pregnant or nursing without medical guidance. Keep out of reach of children.
  • Never stop or change your medication on your own.
